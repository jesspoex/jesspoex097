# Privacy Policy — Tidy Tank

**Effective date:** 2026-08-09
**App:** Tidy Tank (Android)
**Publisher:** jesspoex

## 1. Overview
Tidy Tank is a relaxing ball-sort puzzle game for players aged 13 and up. We
respect your privacy and collect only the minimum data needed to operate the
app and show ads.

## 2. Data we collect
- **Advertising ID** (Android Advertising ID) — used by Google AdMob to serve
  ads. You can reset or opt out via your device's Ads settings.
- **Game progress** (levels, coins, collected fish, streak) — stored **locally
  on your device only**. We do not upload it to any server.
- **Crash/performance logs** — collected by Google Play / AdMob only if you
  opt in to sharing data with them.

We do **not** collect names, emails, precise location, contacts, microphone,
camera, or any personally identifiable information.

## 3. Ads and third parties
We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may use the Advertising ID and
cookies/SDK storage to personalize or measure ads. For users in the EU/EEA/UK,
ads are shown only after consent is collected via Google's User Messaging
Platform (UMP). You can change your choice anytime in your device settings.

- Google Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
- AdMob policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## 4. Children
Tidy Tank is rated **Teen (13+)**. It is not directed to children under 13, and
we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## 5. Data retention
Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app. Advertising
data is handled by Google per their policy.

## 6. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, or object
to processing of your data. To exercise these, contact us and/or use your
device's privacy controls.
